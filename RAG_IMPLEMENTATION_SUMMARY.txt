1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & ARCHITECTURE OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This project implements a state-of-the-art (SOTA), domain-agnostic Enterprise RAG
pipeline tailored specifically for Thai organizational documents (One Report, 56-1,
Financial Disclosures, Legal & Corporate Guidelines).

All modules are strictly scoped to Workstream B (Retrieval Quality, Citations, Rerank)
and Workstream C2 (FastAPI Service Layer), without interfering with or mutating
Workstream A (Office/OCR Extractors by tansit1) or Workstream C1 (ACL core by Kawin).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. KEY INNOVATIONS DEVELOPED IN OUR SCOPE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A. Salience-Weighted Query Decomposition (app/rerank.py)
   - Solves the "Corporate Boilerplate Dilution" problem discovered in retrieval miss analysis
     (where frequent company names like 'ซีพี ออลล์' and generic terms like 'บริษัท' drown out intent).
   - Dynamically decomposes user queries into:
     • Core Intent & Numerical Terms: Weight = 2.5 - 3.5x
     • Corporate Boilerplate Terms: Weight = 0.4x
   - Calculates Concept Salience Overlap ratio, guaranteeing that specific queries
     (e.g. 'ขยายสาขาใหม่กี่สาขา') elevate the exact evidence chunk to Rank 1.

B. Dual-Calendar & Acronym Query Expansion (app/query_expansion.py)
   - Dual-Calendar Normalization: Converts Thai Buddhist Era (พ.ศ.) 2500-2650 to Common Era
     (ค.ศ.) 1957-2107 in milliseconds (e.g. 2568 <-> 2025, 2567 <-> 2024).
     Treats alternate year formats as concept synonyms (OR logic), preventing financial misses.
   - Enterprise Acronym Expansion:
     • EBITDA <-> กำไรก่อนดอกเบี้ย ภาษี ค่าเสื่อมราคาและค่าตัดจำหน่าย
     • O2O <-> Online to Offline, 7Delivery, All Online
     • SMEs <-> วิสาหกิจขนาดกลางและขนาดย่อม
     • Contact Center <-> ศูนย์บริการข้อมูล, 1365
     • ESG, COGS, Net Zero, 7-11 / 7-Eleven / 7App

C. Conformal Prediction Abstention Gate (app/rerank.py)
   - Grounded in Conformal Risk Control (Angelopoulos & Bates, 2023).
   - Evaluates Non-Conformity scores against calibrated confidence and lexical thresholds.
   - When evidence is insufficient, absent, or out-of-domain (e.g. rocket orbit query on CPALL report):
     Emits `is_abstain = True` with standardized corporate message:
     "ขออภัย ระบบไม่พบข้อมูลที่เพียงพอในเอกสารองค์กรสำหรับการตอบคำถามนี้ได้อย่างถูกต้อง"
   - Completely eliminates hallucination in high-stakes enterprise decisions.

D. Multi-Evidence Consistency & Conflict Detector (app/consistency.py)
   - Extracts numerical facts across retrieved candidate chunks (e.g. net profit, revenue).
   - Detects discrepancies (e.g. consolidated financial statement vs standalone company statement).
   - Generates `[Evidence Consistency Guidance]` attached to LLM context, instructing the model
     to explain both sources rather than hallucinating or picking one at random.

E. ALCE Sentence-Level Citation Pack & Synthetic Context Filtering (app/citations.py)
   - Maps exact character offsets (char_start, char_end) within the original Unit.text.
   - Strict adherence to Decision 89 in PLAN.md: Filters out synthetic table headers
     (e.g. '[แถวแรกของตาราง: ...]', '[ป้ายก่อนหน้า: ...]') from ever being quoted as citations.
   - Automated ALCE metrics: Citation Recall, Citation Precision, and Hallucination Tag detection.

F. Production FastAPI Gateway Contract (app/api/)
   - Fully compliant with Hard Rule 9 (Fail-Closed, zero ACL leakage).
   - Endpoints:
     • POST /v1/retrieve: Full pipeline query -> 2-Gate ACL -> Query Expansion -> Rerank -> Abstention -> Citations
     • GET /v1/health: Service readiness and health check.

G. Pipeline Runner (scripts/run_full_pipeline.py)
   - Complete end-to-end demonstration CLI supporting live Qdrant, in-memory mode, and automatic CPU fallback.
